### Instructions for formatting the Cover Page in Word / LaTeX:

1. **Alignment:** Center all elements except the "Submitted by" and "Supervised by" block, which can be centered as a block or aligned left in the lower-middle third.
2. **Typography:**
   - University/Department: **12pt Sans-Serif (Arial/Helvetica/Inter)**, regular or semi-bold, uppercase.
   - Project Type: **14pt Sans-Serif**, bold, uppercase.
   - Title: **22pt to 24pt Sans-Serif**, bold, centered. Consider using a slightly larger line height (1.3) so it reads cleanly.
   - Personal details: **12pt**, regular, line spacing 1.5.
   - Date: **12pt**, regular, centered at the very bottom.
3. **Spacing:** Leave generous vertical spacing between elements to give the page a clean, professional aesthetic.
4. **Logo:** Place your university logo at the top center, immediately above the department name. Keep the vertical height of the logo to around 4–5 cm.
